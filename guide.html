Doodlepad Guide

Everything Doodlepad does, step by step.

Quick start

  1. On the home screen, tap ＋ on the New board card.
  2. Draw with the pen. Two fingers move and zoom the board.
  3. Tap the pen again for its color, size and tip.
  4. Hold anything with a finger to select it, then drag it, or let go for Copy, Delete and more.

The basics

  • The toolbar (at the bottom in portrait, in the top bar in landscape): Pen, Marker, Eraser, Text, Select and Hand, then the red ＋. Tap a tool to use it. Tap it again to show or hide its settings. Once you draw, erase or move the board for about a second, the settings close by themselves so they are out of the way; tap the tool again to bring them back. In portrait, the arrow at the end tucks the toolbar away.
  • Move, zoom and turn: two fingers move, pinch and turn the board. The Hand tool moves it with one finger. Hold that finger still, then swipe up to zoom in or down to zoom out. Double tap with the Hand to jump to 100% or 200%. Zoom goes from 5% to 4000%.
  • Zoom and angle show in the top bar. Tap the zoom to go to 100%, or, at 100%, to see everything (on a board with sheets: a whole sheet). Tap the angle to turn the board upright. Hold the angle to lock turning.
  • Undo and redo: the arrows at the top. Or tap the board with two fingers to undo, with three to redo.
  • Select anything by holding it with a finger. See Selecting and moving.
  • The top bar: back to your boards, zoom and angle, undo, redo, Layers, and ⋯ for the board's name, paper, export and Settings.
  • Tips: a short tip shows the first time you get to a tool or a feature. Each one shows once.
  • Doodlepad needs iOS 17 or later, on iPhone or iPad.

Boards, paper and pages

Make a board

  • On the home screen, tap the yellow ＋ on the New board card. The board opens with the paper you picked last.
  • To pick another paper, tap the bottom of the card: Endless (no edges), or sheets in A4, A5, Letter, 16:9 or Card. Tick Landscape for sheets lying on their side. A board keeps its sheet size.
  • With Pro, From a PDF makes a board from a PDF file: each PDF page becomes a sheet you can write on. The PDF itself is never changed.

Paper

  • Open ⋯. The paper is in the Paper part.
  • Pattern: Plain, Dots, Lines, Grid or Math.
  • Color: Cream, White, Gray, Yellow or Blue. On a PDF board, the color shows only on blank pages.
  • The pattern grows and shrinks with the zoom, so your writing stays on its line.

Pages (boards with sheets)

  • Tap + Page under the last sheet to add one. With Pro, + PDF next to it adds the pages of a PDF.
  • Each sheet has buttons at its top right: move it up or down, add a page below, duplicate it, and ✕ to delete it (tap ✕, then Delete).
  • Each sheet's number is at its top left.
  • The pen draws only on the sheets. Things you move stay on a sheet; drag one past the edge and it goes to the next sheet.

Pen and marker

The pen's settings

Tap the Pen when it's already picked to open its tab:

  • Color: ready colors, and Your color to make any color (Hue, Saturation, Shade, or a hex code). Save colors with ＋ under Saved. Hold a saved color, then tap Delete to remove it.
  • Size and Opacity. Sizes can be saved with ＋ too.
  • Tip: Fine, Ballpoint, Fountain or Pencil. With an Apple Pencil, pressing harder draws thicker (Ballpoint, Fountain) or darker (Pencil). Fine stays even.
  • Smoothing: Off, Low, Mid or High. Higher smooths shaky lines more.
  • Ruler and Auto-scroll: see Ruler and Auto-scroll.
  • Snap: see below.

Neat shapes

  1. Draw a line, box, triangle or circle with the pen.
  2. Keep the pen still at the end for a moment.
  3. It snaps into a clean shape. Keep the pen down and move it to resize or turn the shape. On a straight line, move it to aim the line.

While you hold a line, its length and angle show. Near the edge of the screen, the board scrolls along.

Don't want it? Switch off Snap in the pen's tab.

The marker

A see-through highlighter, with its own color, size and opacity. It works with the ruler and auto-scroll too.

Eraser

  • Pick the Eraser and drag over the ink you want gone. The eraser takes only ink, never notes, shapes or pictures.
  • Erase: Area cuts away only the part you rub; Whole stroke takes a whole line at once.
  • Layers: All layers or Active layer only. When it's set to the active layer, the Eraser button shows a small orange mark.
  • Size: how big the eraser's circle is. It stays the same size at any zoom.
  • Hidden or locked layers, and locked items, are never erased.

Selecting and moving

With any tool

  • Hold a note, shape, picture or a line of ink with a finger to select it, and drag to move it. (Not with the Hand, and not with the Apple Pencil.) This needs Hold to pick on in Settings, which it is at first.
  • Hold empty space, then drag over things to select several at once.
  • Tap Done (✕), or tap empty space, to let go.
  • Drag near the edge of the screen and the board scrolls along.

The Select tool

  • Lasso: Free (draw a loop around things), Box (drag a box) or Brush (rub over things). Holding empty space with another tool uses the same lasso.
  • Size: how wide the brush is, when the lasso is Brush.
  • Selects: Ink and objects, Ink only, or Objects only.
  • Layers: All layers, or Active layer only.
  • Multi-select: while something is selected, a tap adds more to it, or takes it out again.

Resize and turn

  • Drag a corner to resize, a side to stretch one way, and the round knob under it to turn.
  • The lock knob over the box keeps the proportions while you resize. Texts and formulas always keep theirs.
  • Ink lines grow and shrink with the selection. To keep their width, turn on Keep line width in the action bar.
  • A line or an arrow has no box: drag either end.
  • Selected things line up with what's around them (red guide lines). Turn it off in Settings → Line up with other items.

Change what's selected

  • Selected ink: its tab changes the color, size and opacity of the lines.
  • Several of one kind (shapes, or texts and stickies) selected together: their tab changes all of them at once.
  • One selected object shows its own tab: see Text, Shapes, Pictures and Math.

The action bar and the hold menu

The action bar

While something is selected, the toolbar shows what you can do with it:

  • Done and Multi-select.
  • For one kind of thing: Edit text, Edit formula, Remove background (a picture), Add sticky note (a mind map node) or Detach from map (a sticky hanging on a map).
  • Keep line width (with ink), Flip horizontally and Flip vertically.
  • Bring to front and Send to back. Both stay inside the item's layer.
  • Delete.

The hold menu

  • Hold what's selected and let go: Copy, Duplicate, Group or Ungroup, AI image (on devices with Image Playground), Lock, Cut and Delete.
  • A group is selected and moved as one.
  • Lock keeps an item from being moved, changed or erased. A locked item still selects with a hold: then Unlock or Copy.
  • Hold empty space: Paste right there, and Select all. Copies work between boards too. Text or a picture copied in another app shows Paste text or Paste picture.

Text and sticky notes

  • A text box: pick the Text tool and tap the board for a new box, or drag to make an area of your size. Then tap Edit text or tap the box again to type. A text box left empty goes away.
  • Type in a text or a sticky: select it and tap Edit text, or double tap it with Select or Text. Done typing puts the keyboard away.
  • The text tab:
    • Color: Text for the letters, Note for the background. A text with a Note color becomes a sticky note.
    • Font: Rounded, Clean, Hand or Marker.
    • Size of the letters.
    • Style: bold, italic, and left, center or right.
  • A sticky gets taller when its text no longer fits.
  • Resize a text box with a corner: the letters keep their size and wrap inside it. To change their size, use Size.

The ＋ button

Tap the red ＋ in the toolbar to add:

  • Shape: see Shapes.
  • Sticky: a note to write on.
  • Mind map: see Mind maps.
  • Image from your photos, or Camera for a new photo.
  • AI image, on devices with Image Playground: see Pictures.
  • Math (Pro): see Math formulas.

New things come in selected, in the middle of the screen, so you can move them right away.

Shapes

  • ＋ → Shape: Line, Arrow, Box, Circle, Triangle, Star and Bubble.
  • Color: Outline and Fill, each with its own color or None. The link button keeps the fill the same as the outline. A slider sets how see-through the fill is.
  • Width of the outline.
  • Corners rounds a box or a bubble. Sides turns a triangle into a shape with up to 12 sides. Points sets a star's points. Ends puts arrowheads on a line: None, End, Start or Both.

Pictures

  • Add one with ＋ → Image or ＋ → Camera.
  • AI image: ＋ → AI image makes a new picture with Apple's Image Playground. With things selected, AI image in the hold menu starts from them.
  • AI image needs iOS 18.1 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or newer, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro chip or newer. On other devices the button doesn't show.
  • Remove background in the action bar keeps only the subject of a photo. It runs on your device.
  • The picture tab: Opacity.

Mind maps

  1. Tap ＋ → Mind map. The first node is the main idea; double tap it to type.
  2. Select a node: the yellow ＋ adds a node under it, the green ＋ adds one beside it. Return while typing adds the next one.
  3. Drag a node to move it with everything hanging off it. Drop it on another node to hang it there instead.
  4. Add sticky note in the action bar hangs a note on the node. Drag a sticky onto a node to hang it there; Detach from map takes it off.
  5. Deleting a node with branches asks: This node (its branches move up) or Whole branch.

Math formulas (Pro)

  • ＋ → Math opens the formula editor.
  • Build the formula with the buttons, in tabs: Recent, Basic, Greek, Symbols, Calculus and Ready. Next and Back move between the empty boxes. You can type LaTeX too.
  • Tap Add. Resize the formula with a corner; it stays sharp. Its tab changes the Color. To change the formula, select it and tap Edit formula.

Ruler

  • Turn it on with Ruler in the pen's (or marker's) tab.
  • Start a line at its edge for a straight line. The length shows in centimeters above the tools.
  • Drag its middle to move it. Two fingers turn it too. The turn knob turns it around the red dot.
  • The ruler always stops on a whole degree, so the angle it shows is exact. At 0°, 45°, 90° and 135° you feel a tick.
  • Lock it with the lock on the ruler: it stays in its place on the board, and a line can start anywhere along it.

Auto-scroll

For long notes: the board slides on while you write.

  • Open it with Auto-scroll in the pen's (or marker's) tab. Or hold the Pen or Marker button to switch it on or off. A blue arrow on the button shows it's on.
  • Mode: Off, At the edge (slides once your writing gets near the right edge) or Every stroke (slides a moment after each stroke, so you write in one spot).
  • Trigger (At the edge): Early, Normal or Late. Wait (Every stroke): Long, Normal or Short.
  • Where it slides to: while these settings are open, a red line on the board shows where your writing ends up after a slide. Drag the knob on the line to move it, from the left edge to a bit before the middle of the screen. The settings stay open while you write, so you can try it.
  • Undo right after a slide moves the board back first; the next undo takes back your writing.
  • Buttons: New line shows a button that takes you to the start of the next line. Scroll shows ◀ ▶ on the right edge to slide the board by hand.

Apple Pencil

  • On iPad, Settings → Draw with finger sets what a finger does. Turn it off so that only the Pencil draws, and a finger moves the board, so your hand can rest on the screen.
  • Pressing harder draws thicker or darker, depending on the pen's tip. The Apple Pencil (USB-C) doesn't sense pressure, so its lines stay even.
  • Holding to select works with a finger, not with the Pencil.
  • Double tap the Pencil (2nd generation or Pro) to do what you chose in the iPad's Settings → Apple Pencil: switch to the eraser and back, switch to the last tool, or open the color or size settings. The Pencil Pro's squeeze works the same way, with its own choice there.
  • Hover: on an iPad that senses the Pencil above the screen, a dot shows where the pen will draw, in its color and size. With the eraser, its circle shows.

Layers

Layers keep things apart, for example a sketch under your notes. More than one layer is Pro; a free board has one.

  • Tap Layers in the top bar. The highlighted layer is the active one: new ink and objects go there. Tap a layer to make it active. Tap the small pencil by its name to rename it.
  • New adds a layer. Drag a layer's handle to move it up or down.
  • Each layer's buttons: lock (nothing on it can be drawn on, selected or erased), hide and delete.
  • Duplicate layer and Merge down (into the layer below). Hold a layer's name to select everything on it.
  • While something is selected, Move picked to … puts it into the active layer.

Export

  • Open ⋯, then Export image (endless boards) or Export PDF (boards with sheets). Save, print or send it from the share sheet.
  • Hidden layers stay out of exports.
  • ⋯ also has Clear board, which takes everything off the board. Undo brings it back.

The home screen

  • Hold a board, or tap its ⋯, for its menu: Rename, Duplicate, Move to folder and Delete.
  • Folders: tap Folder ＋ in the row at the top, or New folder in Move to folder. Hold a folder, or tap it when it's already open, to rename it, give it a color or delete it. Deleting a folder keeps its boards.
  • Search at the top. Sort by Last edited, Date created or Name.
  • Recently Deleted (the 🗑 in the folder row): deleted boards stay there for 30 days. Tap one to Restore it, or Delete now to remove it for good.
  • Inside a board, ⋯ shows its name (tap the pencil to rename it), when it was made and changed, its size, and its pages.

Settings

On a board, open ⋯ and tap the gear. Settings are for each device. Guide at the top opens this page.

Touch

  • Draw with finger (iPad): see Apple Pencil.
  • Two-finger tap to undo.
  • Lock rotation: two fingers don't turn the board.
  • Hold to pick: holding an item selects it.

While drawing

  • Line up with other items: red guide lines while you move things.
  • Hide bars sideways: on a phone held sideways, the top bar hides once you draw. A button brings it back.
  • Keep screen on, Drawing sound and Haptics (iPhone).

Doodlepad Pro

Tap the ⭐ on the home screen. Pro is a subscription, yearly or monthly. The yearly plan may start with a free week. Pro gives you:

  • Unlimited boards (3 are free; boards in Recently Deleted don't count).
  • Boards from PDFs, and + PDF on a board.
  • More than one layer on a board.
  • Math formulas.

Everything else is free. Without Pro, your boards and layers keep working. If you bought Pro once in an earlier version of Doodlepad, you keep it.

New device, or reinstalled the app? Tap the ⭐, then Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account. To cancel, use Manage subscription on the same screen, or your Apple Account's subscriptions in the Settings app.
