Questions

  • Where are my boards from version 1? Doodlepad 2.0 is a new app inside, and it can't open boards made in version 1. They aren't deleted. Write to me and I'll help.
  • Do I need an Apple Pencil? No. A finger draws too. On iPad, to draw only with the Pencil and move the board with a finger, turn off ⋯ → Settings → Draw with finger.
  • Why can't I make a fourth board? The free version has 3 boards. Pro has as many as you like. Your boards always keep working, with or without Pro.
  • How do I get a PDF of my notes? On a board with sheets, open ⋯ → Export PDF. An endless board exports as a picture with ⋯ → Export image.
  • My writing shows up on the wrong layer. New ink goes into the active layer (the highlighted one in Layers). Select what you wrote, then tap "Move picked to …" in Layers.
  • Can I undo? Yes: the arrows at the top, or tap with two fingers (three fingers redo).

Doodlepad Pro

Pro is a subscription, yearly or monthly. It gives you unlimited boards (3 are free), boards made from a PDF, more than one layer, and math formulas. If you bought Pro once in version 1, you keep it.

  • New device, or reinstalled the app? Tap the ⭐ on the home screen, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Cancel with Manage subscription on the same screen, or in the Settings app under your name → Subscriptions, at least a day before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Lost a board?

Deleted boards wait in Recently Deleted on the home screen for 30 days. Tap the board there, then Restore.
