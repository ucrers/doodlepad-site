Getting started

A short guide opens the first time you start Doodlepad. You can open it again any time in Settings → Help (the gear on the home screen).

Doodlepad Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. It unlocks unlimited boards (3 are free), boards made from a PDF, more than one layer, and math formulas.

  • Bought Pro on another device, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → Doodlepad Pro → Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud sync

  • Sign in with the same Apple Account on your iPhone and iPad.
  • Make sure iCloud is on for Doodlepad, in the Settings app under your name → iCloud.
  • New boards can take a minute to appear on your other device. Opening the app there helps.
  • Without iCloud, Doodlepad keeps working on each device on its own.

Lost a board?

Deleted boards wait in Recently Deleted on the home screen for 7, 30 or 90 days (you choose in Settings). Open it and tap Restore.
